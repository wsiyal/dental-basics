# Dental Basics — Implants & Veneers Exam Guide

A single-page, self-contained study guide covering dental implants and veneers,
written in plain language for someone coming to the subject from zero.

**Read it here → https://wsiyal.github.io/dental-basics/**

## What's in it

- **Foundations** — tooth anatomy, tooth types, the FDI numbering system,
  X-ray vs CBCT, the cavity → treatment ladder, materials, procedure terminology
- **Implants** — what an implant is, indications and contraindications, bone
  grafts and sinus lifts, the full procedure, All-on-4 / All-on-6, aftercare,
  and the major brands
- **Veneers** — indications, the ten production steps, placement, brands,
  translucency and shades, aftercare
- **A self-marking question bank** — 237 core questions plus a 268-item FDI
  mastery lab, with running accuracy scoring

Every figure and photograph is inlined as base64, so `index.html` works on its
own: email it, drop it on a USB stick, or open it offline. No build step, no
dependencies, no network calls.

## Features

- Collapsible sections with expand/collapse-all, remembered between visits
- Multiple-choice and true/false cards that score themselves
- Two appearances — a Studio look and an Apple (iOS/macOS) Liquid Glass look —
  with an Auto setting that follows the platform
- Light and dark themes
- Accessibility fallbacks for Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast, Reduce
  Motion and forced colours; every navigation surface measures at least 4.5:1
  contrast in both themes
- Responsive from 375 px phones up, with safe-area insets for notched devices

## Accuracy

The FDI reference chart is generated from code rather than drawn by hand, so
every one of the 32 codes is derived from the same dictionaries the question
bank uses and cannot drift. Quadrants follow the standard: Q1 upper right,
Q2 upper left, Q3 lower left, Q4 lower right — so 11 is the upper right central
incisor, 21 the upper left, 31 the lower left, 41 the lower right.

Illustrations marked as such are illustrations, not clinical records, and are
captioned that way in the guide.
